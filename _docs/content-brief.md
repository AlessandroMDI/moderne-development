## What MDI Does (Use This for "About" and Hero Copy)

MDI is building a next-generation Operating System (MDOS) for Developers, Builders, and the whole Architectural, Engineering, Construction (AEC) industry. The platform uses digital construction workflows, AI-enabled robots, and Construction Tech (ConTech) integrated into proven building systems through a scalable development ecosystem — an end-to-end build stack that takes a project from concept to completion.

MDOS is not a product MDI sells separately — it is the platform MDI is building and licensing to developers and builders. MDI and MDOS are essentially the same thing: the company's entire value proposition is the platform.

MDI operates from the **developer's vantage point**, joint-venturing with builders and developers early on to validate designs, BIM assets, technology integrations, and construction processes in real-world conditions. Unlike architects, contractors, or materials suppliers, MDI controls the full lifecycle — which is the differentiating argument.

**The differentiating argument (use verbatim or close to it):**
> "Only developers can truly drive construction innovation, because they control the full lifecycle from land to delivery."
